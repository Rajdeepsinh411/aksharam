Step 1 · Preparing for the scan

Calm, comfortable and quick.

Our trained staff explain each step before you begin and help you settle comfortably on the scan table. Most scans take only a few minutes, and someone is always close by.

Step 2 · Scanning

16-slice MDCT, including HRCT chest.

Our 16-slice multi-detector CT captures fine cross-sectional images within seconds. It supports a wide range of studies, including high-resolution CT (HRCT) of the chest, as advised by your doctor.

Step 3 · Slices to image

Detailed images, read by qualified radiologists.

Each thin slice becomes part of one detailed picture. Our radiologists review the images carefully and prepare a clear report for your doctor.

Step 4 · Ultrasound

Sonography, 3D/4D and colour Doppler.

Ultrasound uses sound waves, not radiation. We offer abdomen, pelvis and obstetric sonography, 3D/4D sonography for detailed foetal anatomy assessment, and colour Doppler to study blood flow.

Step 5 · Report ready

Your report, without the wait.

We work to share reports promptly, and our team will tell you when yours will be ready. Urgent and emergency cases are given priority.
