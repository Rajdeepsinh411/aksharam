About Aksharam Diagnostics

Aksharam Diagnostics is a radiology and diagnostic imaging centre in Viramgam that serves patients from the town and the surrounding villages. We now also welcome patients at our branch in South Bopal, Ahmedabad.

Our focus is simple: accurate imaging and caring service. With a 16-slice MDCT scanner, digital X-ray, sonography and colour Doppler, we aim to make advanced diagnostics available locally, so families do not have to travel far for the tests their doctor advises.

Every study is reviewed by a qualified radiologist, and our team takes time to explain preparation, answer questions and keep you comfortable from arrival to report.

  • 16-slice MDCT scanner
  • Digital X-ray
  • Ultrasound with colour Doppler
  • 24-hour emergency imaging
